## FAQ

#### What Should I put in Header Prefix Section ?

Personaly i have found putting 510 works perfectly well for any item 

#### Where can i find the EAN-13 Barcode Numbers?

For finding the barcode numbers you can either go to asda and look at any barcode which you will then see a number that is your EAN-13, or alternatively you can find your barcode numbers on https://www.prodlookup.co.uk/


#### How do i test my barcodes before actually scanning?
For this i would recommend downloading the Asda Scan and go app which is avalible on Android or IOS, You would then proceed to gen the barcode on a different device seperate to the one your scanning on and point it at the barcode the website made and if it scans correctly it should be added to your basket for this though you will need another device to display the barcode on but once you have verified it works you can gen it on your phone and scan it directly at the till.


#### How do i use the barcode generated by the website?
To use the generated barcodes you have 2 options, either you can go into asda and use their handsets provided near the front of the store and scan the barcode on your phone made by the webiste **NOT THE ONE ON THE ACTUAL ITEM**, you then find the item you generated the discounted barcode for and carry it to the scan and go checkout in the store and pay for your items, The other option is scanning the discounted barcode at the physical till instead of the one on the item and place it on the weighing scales as normal and then you pay.

## Requirements (Mobile App)
- **A Apple Device With Developer Mode Enabled**
- **IOS -17 Or Newer**
- **A Sideloading App / Client Such as - Sideloady - Sidestore | Or a On-Phone Signer Such As Feather, Ksign Or Esign**
- **A Apple Developer Certificate (Note: This is not mandatory as apple allows self signing with your own Apple ID but it is recommended to avoid Expiration )**
- **Internet Connection (Only For Installation)**
